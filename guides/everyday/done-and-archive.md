# Done cards and the archive

Finished work piles up. Kanbanto keeps it out of your way in two steps: older done cards are tucked behind a line,
and when you no longer need them on the board at all, you archive them.

## The Done list shows recent cards

A list for finished work shows only the cards finished or touched in the last 14 days. The rest sit behind a line at
the top of the list.

![A Done list with "5 older", Show and Archive](/images/done-list.webp)

(The ring in the picture is on **Archive…**, which is used further down this page.)

- **Show** reveals the older cards for now. **Hide** tucks them away again.
- To change the number of days, or always show everything: **Display → Done lists → All** or **Recent**.

The Outline and Timeline do it differently: one **Hide done** switch covers both of them (a button on the Timeline,
and in **Display** on the Outline), with a line saying how many are hidden.

## Archive a card

Archiving puts a card away without deleting it. It leaves the board, its counts and its searches, and keeps
everything: description, comments, files and subtasks.

Open a card's **⋯** menu:

- **Archive** puts it away as it is.
- **Complete and archive** finishes it and its subtasks first, so it is remembered as completed.

An **Undo** button appears for a few seconds.

## Archive older done cards in one go

1. Open the Done list's **⋯** menu and choose **Archive older cards…**, or click **Archive…** beside "older".
2. Choose how long ago: "Cards done more than **14** days ago". Change the number to suit.
3. Check the count, then click the button, which says how many: "Archive 5 cards".

![The Archive older cards dialog, saying how many cards will go](/images/archive-older.webp)

The dialog tells you how many cards will go before anything happens. **Undo** brings them all back.

![The message after archiving, with Undo ringed](/images/archive-3-undo.webp)

Two things to know about what it picks:

- It counts from the day a card was **done**.
- It archives whole finished cards with their subtasks. A finished subtask whose parent is not finished yet stays
  where it is, so the unfinished work keeps its history. The dialog says how many of those there are.

## Find and restore archived cards

Open the board's **⋯** menu and choose **Archived cards**. The number beside it is how many the board has.

![The board's menu, with Archived cards ringed](/images/archive-4-menu.webp)

![The archived cards, with Restore ringed](/images/archive-5-restore.webp)

- Each card says when it was archived, from which list, and whether it was completed.
- Click one to read it, with the subtasks archived with it.
- **Restore** puts it back where it was. If its list has since been deleted, it goes to the first list of the same
  kind.
- The **bin** at the end of a row deletes the card for good. This is the one action here that cannot be undone.

This is the same page as **Search all cards**, with **Archived** switched on. So you can search by words, narrow
with **Any status** (Done, or Not done), and use the first box to look at another board, or all of them at once.

## Archive a whole board

A board's owner can archive it from **⋯ → Board settings**. It leaves the boards page (it is kept under "Archived
boards" at the bottom), becomes read-only for everyone, and can be restored any time.

## Good to know

- Archiving makes a big board quicker to open.
- Anyone who can edit the board can archive and restore cards.
- Board stats still count archived cards, so your history stays whole.
- Your AI assistant can still find archived cards when you ask what was done in a period.
