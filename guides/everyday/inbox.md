# Your Inbox

Your Inbox is the place for things that have no board yet: a thought in the middle of other work, something to do
later, a note from a call. It is one click away beside whatever board is open, and only you can see it.

## Open it

Click the **tray** in the top bar, or press <kbd>I</kbd>. The Inbox opens at the left and the board moves over: both
stay usable. The number beside the tray says how many cards wait in it.

![The Inbox open beside a board](/images/inbox-1-open.webp)

It stays open as you go from board to board, and on your boards page, until you close it with **✕** or click the
tray again. Each device remembers whether you left it open.

## Add a card

Click **Add a card**, type a title and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The box stays open for the next one. Pressing
<kbd>I</kbd> puts the cursor there, so a note takes a second: <kbd>I</kbd>, type, <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

A date typed in the title is understood here too ("call the bank tomorrow 10am"), as when you
[add a card to a list](/everyday/cards#add-a-card).

An Inbox card is a card like any other. Click it to open it: it has a description, a due date, reminders, subtasks,
files and comments.

## Its lists

The Inbox is a board of your own, shown as a stack: a section for each of its lists, **To Do**, **Doing** and
**Done** to begin with.

- Click a section's name to fold it away, or open it again. **Done** starts folded.
- Drag a card to another section to change its list, or up and down to put it in order.
- The **⋯** at a card's corner does the same without dragging, and has **Complete and archive** and **Archive**.

So a small thing never has to go on a board: note it, do it, finish it, all from here.

To rename the lists, add one, or see the Inbox as a Board, a Timeline or an Outline, click **Open as a board** (the
arrow at the top of the panel). There it works like any board of yours.

## Put a card on a board

Open the board, then drag the card out of the Inbox onto one of its lists. It lands where you drop it.

![A card dragged from the Inbox onto a list](/images/inbox-2-drag.webp)

- On the **Timeline**, the **Outline**, or a Board tab that is not showing lists as its columns, drop the card
  anywhere on the view. It goes to the list with the same name as the one it was in, or else the first list of the
  same kind (not started, in progress, done).
- Without dragging: click the card's **⋯**, then **Move to “the open board”…** to choose the list first, or **Move to
  another board…** for any board you can add to.

![An Inbox card's menu](/images/inbox-3-menu.webp)

The card takes its subtasks, comments and files with it. Its labels are matched by name, and added to the board
when it has none by that name. A value in one of [your own fields](/everyday/fields) comes along only if the board
uses that field: when something is left behind, the message that follows the move says what.

A move to another board is not undone with <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>. To take it back, move the card again: its **⋯**, **Move
to another board…**, **Inbox**.

## Send a card to the Inbox

From any board: click the card's **⋯**, then **Move to another board…**. Your Inbox is first in the list.

## On a phone

The tray opens the Inbox over the board. Press and hold a card to drag it between sections. To put a card on the
board behind it, use the card's **⋯** and **Move to “the open board”…**.

![The Inbox on a phone](/images/inbox-4-phone.webp)

## Good to know

- **Only you.** The Inbox cannot be shared, moved to a workspace, archived or deleted, and nobody else can open it.
  You can rename it; the tray and the panel still say Inbox.
- **Everyone has one.** It is made the first time you open Kanbanto.
- **It is not a tile on your boards page.** The tray is its place. Star it (the board's name → **Add to
  favourites**) and it shows with your favourites too.
- **Assistants use it.** Ask an assistant to "remind me to call the bank" without naming a board, and the card goes
  to your Inbox. See [what to ask](/ai/what-to-ask).
- **Search cards** and **My week** include its cards, with those of your other Personal boards.
- **Undo.** <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> takes back your last change, whether you made it on the board or in the Inbox.
