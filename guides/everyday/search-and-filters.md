# Search and filters

Three tools narrow down what you see: **search** finds words, **filters** show a kind of card, and **Search cards**
looks across every board.

## Search this board

Click **Search tasks** at the top, or press <kbd>/</kbd>, and type.

![Searching a board for "launch"](/images/search.webp)

The board shows only cards whose title or description has your words. Clear the box to see everything again.

If archived cards match too, a line under the bar says how many, as a link to them.

## Filter

Click **Filter**.

![The Filter menu](/images/filter.webp)

Tick what you want to see. Choices in one group widen the result (Ann *or* Ben); different groups narrow it (Ann's
cards *that are* urgent).

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Recently changed** | Cards moved, edited or commented on in the last few days. Turn it on and a box appears for the number of days. |
| **No activity lately** | Cards nothing has happened on for a number of days. Good for finding stuck work. |
| **Status** | Cards in the lists you tick. |
| **Assignee** | One or more people's cards, or **No one assigned**. |
| **Priority** | Urgent, High, Medium, Low, or **No priority**. |
| **Labels** | Cards with a label. |
| **Fields** | Cards by one of the board's [own fields](/everyday/fields#filter-by-a-field): a stage, an amount, a yes or no. |

Filters apply to all three views: Board, Timeline and Outline. Small tags under the bar show which filters are on;
click the ✕ on one to drop that filter.

## Presets: save a combination

When a mix of filters and Display options is one you come back to, save it.

1. Set the filters and Display the way you want.
2. Click **Presets**, then **Save this view as a new preset…** and give it a name, such as "Mine this week" or
   "Review".

![The Presets menu](/images/presets.webp){.medium}

Everyone on the board can pick a preset. People who can edit the board can save, update, rename and delete them.

## Search cards: every board at once

**Search cards** looks through all the boards you can open. Find it on the boards page, or as **Search all cards**
in the menu under your initials.

![The Search cards page, showing archived cards](/images/archived-cards.webp)

It can:

- search **words** in titles, descriptions and comments,
- show cards **on their boards**, **archived** ones, or both,
- narrow by **board** or workspace, **person**, **priority**, **label**, **due date** and one of your
  [own fields](/everyday/fields) (some of these are under **More**),
- start from a ready-made search: **My tasks**, **Following** (the cards you are
  [told about](/people/notifications#cards-you-follow)), **Done this week**, **Recently changed**, **Archived**,
- and look at a **stretch of time**: cards done, made, changed or archived today, this week, last month, or between
  two days you choose.

This is the place to answer "what did I finish last week?" or "where was that card about the printer?". The address
of the page holds the whole search, so you can bookmark one or send it to a teammate. They see the same search run
over the boards *they* can open, which may not be the same cards.

Click a result to see the card right there. To change it, open it on its board.

## Good to know

- Search and filters only change what *you* see. Nobody else's board changes.
- **Display → Card age** adds a small tag to cards that have been quiet: days since anything happened, amber after a
  week and red after two.
