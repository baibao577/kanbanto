# Change several cards at once

Tick a few cards in the [Outline](/views/outline), then move, assign, label, date or archive them together. It is
one change: one **Undo** takes all of it back.

![Three rows ticked in the Outline, and the bar that changes them](/images/several-1-bar.webp)

## Tick the cards

Open the **Outline** tab.

- **Point at a row.** A box appears at its start. Click it to tick the row. Once one row is ticked, every row
  shows its box.
- **A run of rows:** tick the first, then hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> and click the box of the last. Everything between
  is ticked.
- **Everything shown:** the box in the **Task** heading ticks every card in the table. With a search or a filter
  on, that is every card it found, so a filter is the quick way to choose: filter to "Ben, due this week", tick
  the heading's box, and you have them. Click it again to untick them.
- **On a phone or a tablet** there is nothing to point with. On a tablet the boxes are always there; on a phone,
  tap **Select** above the list to show them.

A bar appears at the bottom of the view. It says how many cards are ticked, and stays in sight while you scroll
and tick more. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or the **✕** at the end of the bar, to let go of them all.

Ticking a card that has subtasks ticks **that card only**. To take everything under the ticked cards as well,
click **Add their subtasks** in the bar.

## Change them

| In the bar | What it does |
|---|---|
| **List** | Moves the cards to a list. They arrive at the end of it. |
| **Assign** | Gives them to one person, or to nobody. |
| **Labels** | Adds a label to all of them, or takes it off all of them. See below. |
| **Priority** | Sets a priority, or clears it. |
| **Dates** | Sets a start or a due date, or clears it. |
| **More → Set a field…** | Sets one of the board's [own fields](/everyday/fields): pick the field, then the value, as in a card. |
| **More → Archive** | Puts the cards away, with their subtasks. See [the archive](/everyday/done-and-archive). |
| **More → Delete…** | Deletes them, with their subtasks. Asks first. |

After each change a message says what happened ("Assigned 4 cards to Ben") with an **Undo** button.
<kbd>⌘Z</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> works too. The cards stay ticked, so you can go on: assign them, then give them
a due date.

A tick in a menu shows what all the ticked cards have in common. Cards that already are what you choose are left
alone, and aren't counted in the message.

### Labels: add or remove, the rest stay

![The Labels menu for two cards: a dash beside a label only one of them has](/images/several-2-labels.webp){.medium}

The **Labels** menu lists the board's labels. A **tick** means every ticked card has that label; a **dash** means
some do, and says how many. Click a label to give it to all of them. Click a ticked one to take it off all of
them. Labels you don't touch stay on the cards as they were.

A field that holds several people works the same way: the editor shows who every ticked card has, adding someone
adds them to every card, and removing someone removes them from every card.

### Cards that stay where they are

On a board where a parent's list [follows its subtasks](/everyday/subtasks), a card with subtasks has no list of
its own to set. **List** moves the other ticked cards and the message says how many stayed ("1 card follows its
subtasks and stays"). Move its subtasks instead: tick the card, click **Add their subtasks**, and move them.

## Good to know

- **Who is told.** Someone given many cards at once gets one line under their bell ("assigned “Homepage” and 11
  more cards to you"), not one for each. The same goes for everything a card's followers hear about: more than
  three cards in one change is one line.
- **The selection is yours.** Nobody else sees what you ticked. It is kept while you search, filter, sort or
  [group](/views/outline#group-the-rows) (the bar says how many ticked cards aren't shown just now), and forgotten
  when you leave the Outline or the board.
- **A card that goes** (archived or deleted, by you or by someone else) is no longer ticked, also when an Undo
  brings it back.
- **Grouped**, a card that shows under two headings is ticked under both, and counts once.
- **Who can.** Everyone who can edit the board. Viewers and visitors see no boxes.
- **How many.** Up to 2,000 cards in one change.
- The board's [limits and rules](/views/limits) see the change like any other: a list turns red if it is now over,
  and a rule that tells people says all the cards in one line.
