# Tasks inside tasks

Any card can hold smaller cards, and those can hold more. A project becomes its parts, each part its steps. There is
no limit to how deep it goes.

## Add a subtask

1. Open a card.
2. Under **Subtasks**, click **Add a subtask**.
3. Type its title and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Keep going to add more.

![A card's subtasks, with Add a subtask ringed](/images/sub-1-add.webp)

A subtask is a full card. It has its own list, person, due date, description and comments, and it can have subtasks
of its own. Click one to open it; the trail above its title shows where it belongs, and each name in the trail is a
link back up. The circle beside a subtask shows its status; click it to move the subtask to another list, such as
Done.

## How subtasks show on the board

To begin with, a card that has subtasks is not shown as a card. It becomes a **grey heading** in each list that
holds one of its subtasks, with the subtasks under it. Here "Homepage" appears in To Do, Doing and Done, because it
has a subtask in each:

![A board where the Homepage card shows as a grey heading in each list](/images/board.webp)

- The heading shows progress across all lists, such as **1/3** done.
- **Click the heading** to open the parent card.
- **Drag the heading** to move that list's group of subtasks together.

In **Display** you can change how this looks:

![The Display menu](/images/display.webp)

- **Group subtasks under their parent** is the switch for those headings. Turn it off and the parent is a card like
  any other, with its subtasks as separate cards.
- **Where it belongs** shows the parent's name above each card.
- **Subtask checklist** and **Progress bar** show a parent's steps on the parent card itself.
- **Rows** can give each project or each parent a band across the whole board.

## A parent's status

By default a parent **follows its subtasks**: it is in progress when work on them has started, and done when they
all are. You move the subtasks; the parent takes care of itself.

If you would rather move parents by hand, the board's owner (the person who made it, or anyone they made an owner)
can change this in **⋯ → Board settings**: a parent's status "Follows its subtasks" or "Is set by you".

## Look at one project at a time

Open a card's **⋯** menu and choose **Focus on its subtasks**. The board now shows only that card's subtasks. The bar
under the top shows where you are, with a way back to the whole board.

![A board showing only Homepage's subtasks](/images/sub-2-focus.webp)

## See the whole tree

The **Outline** view shows every task under its parent, like an indented table. Fold and unfold branches, and drag
rows to rearrange them.

![The Outline view, showing tasks nested under their parents](/images/outline.webp)

## Change where a task belongs

- In the open card, under **More**, click **Parent** and pick another card, or none to make it top-level.
- In the Outline, drag the row to its new place.

## Good to know

- Archiving or deleting a card takes its subtasks with it.
- Moving a card to another board takes its subtasks too.
- A card cannot go inside one of its own subtasks.
