# Comment on the text

A comment can be about a few words of an article instead of the whole card: "is this number still right?", next to
the number. Others answer under it, and when it is settled, someone resolves it.

Everyone who can open the board can do this, viewers too. Nobody needs to be able to edit the article.

## Comment on some words

1. Open the article full page: open the card and click **Expand**.
2. Select the words you mean. A **Comment** button appears under them.

   ![Words selected in an article, with the Comment button under them](/images/km-8-select.webp)

3. Click it, write your comment, and press <kbd>⌘Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Enter</kbd>) or click **Comment**.

The words are marked in the text, and your comment sits beside it.

![A comment beside the text, with the words it is about marked and an answer under it](/images/km-9-thread.webp)

- Select a whole sentence, or a whole paragraph, when that is what you mean.
- **@** mentions someone, as in any comment.
- On a phone, select the words the way your phone does (press and hold, then drag the ends). The **Comments**
  button at the top opens the comments over the text.

## Answer and resolve

- **Reply** answers a comment. The answers stay together under the words.
- **Resolve** says the matter is settled. The comment folds away under **Resolved**, and its words are no longer
  marked. Anyone who can comment can resolve.
- **Reopen** brings it back. So does a new answer.

Click marked words to go to their comment. Click a comment's quoted words to go to them in the text.

## The same comments on the card

Every comment on the text is also in the card's **Comments**, with the words quoted above it. It is one
conversation, shown in two places. Click the quoted words there to open the article at them.

![The same comment in the card's Comments, with the words quoted above it](/images/km-10-in-comments.webp){.medium}

## When the text changes

Nothing is written into the article to hold a comment in place. The comment remembers its words, and finds them
again each time the article is shown.

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| The words move: text is added above them, or their paragraph is moved | The comment moves with them. |
| The text around them changes | Nothing. |
| The words themselves are rewritten | The comment says "The text this was about has changed". It stays in Comments, and beside the text under **No longer in the text**. Resolve it if the rewrite settled it. |
| The same words are in the text twice | The comment stays with the ones it was made on, told apart by what stands around them. |

So an assistant can rewrite an article, or five people can [write in it at once](/knowledge/together), and no
comment is lost.

## Who is told

The same people as for any comment:

- everyone who follows the card (commenting makes you follow it), and
- anyone you @mention.

The line under their bell quotes the words, and opens the article at them. When someone else resolves a comment of
yours, you get one line saying so.

## Good to know

- Comments are made while reading. While you write (**Edit**), the marks and the comments stay in view, but
  selecting words doesn't offer **Comment**.
- A visitor with the public link reads the comments and can't add any.
- Deleting a comment on the text deletes the answers to it.
- An earlier version of the article, read under **Versions**, isn't marked.
- The marks need a current browser. In an older one the comments are all there, beside the text and on the card,
  without marks in the text.
- An assistant connected to Kanbanto sees these comments with their words, can add one about words it quotes, and
  can answer. Resolving is for people. See [what to ask](/ai/what-to-ask).
