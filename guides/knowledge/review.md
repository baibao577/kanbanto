# Review and keep it current

Writing an article is half of it. The other half is that someone else reads it before it counts, and that somebody
looks at it again before it goes stale. The board's lists do both.

## From draft to published

| When | Do this | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| You start writing | Put the card in **Drafting**, assigned to you. | Everyone sees it is being written, and by whom. |
| It is ready for a second pair of eyes | Drag it to **In review**. | With a [rule](/views/telling-rules) on that list, the reviewers get a line under their bell. |
| A reviewer has something to say | They comment on the card. | You are told, as its assignee. Viewers can comment too. |
| It is agreed | Drag it to **Published**. Set its **Owner** and a **Review by** date, and take the assignee off. | It is part of the handbook, and nobody's task. |

Small boards can skip **In review**: drag straight to Published and @mention someone in a comment to ask for a
look.

## Review, as a reader

Open the card and click **Expand** to read the article full page. Write what you think in the card's comments:

- Quote the words you mean. Select them in the text, copy, and paste them into your comment after a `>` so they
  show as a quote.
- @mention the writer if they aren't the card's assignee.
- React with 👍 or ✅ when a comment needs no words back.

## Change a published article

Open it and edit it. It doesn't have to go back to Drafting for a small fix.

The card's **History** tab says who edited the description and when, and **Versions** on the full page keeps the
text as it was, to read or to bring back (see [write together](/knowledge/together#versions)). For a change that
matters, say what changed in a comment: everyone who follows the article is told.

To hear about changes to an article you rely on, open it and click **Follow**. See
[notifications](/people/notifications).

## Look at it again

An article is true on the day it was written. **Review by** is the day someone should check it still is.

- Open the **Review overdue** preset (see [set up the board](/knowledge/set-up)), or sort the Outline by
  **Review by**.
- Read each one. Still right? Move the date on. Not right any more? Fix it, or mark it outdated.
- Do it on a day you choose: the first Monday of the month works.

Six months is a good first guess for the date. Prices and anything with a person's name in it need less.

## Mark it outdated

Drag the card to **Outdated**. Say in a comment, or at the top of the text, what replaced it.

Outdated counts as Done, so the card fades from the board after two weeks and its title is struck through in the
Outline. To put it away for good, archive it: see [done cards and the archive](/everyday/done-and-archive). An
archived article can still be found and read in Search cards, and brought back.

## Who does what

| Role on the board | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, and sets up the lists, fields and rules. |
| **Editor** | Write, review, publish and mark outdated. |
| **Viewer** | Read every article, comment, follow. |
| **Anyone with the link** (if switched on) | Read. |

## Good to know

- Two people can edit the same article at the same moment: both are in the same text, and it is saved as they
  write. See [write together](/knowledge/together).
- Moving a card between lists isn't an edit: History keeps the two apart.
- A [limit](/views/limits) on **In review** ("at most 5") keeps reviews from piling up.
