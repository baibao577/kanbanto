# Samples to copy

Three boards to model yours on, and seven outlines for articles. Copy an outline, paste it into a card's
description, and it comes in with its headings and lists.

## Three boards

### A team handbook

How a small team works: who does what, how things are done, what was decided and why.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lists** | Inbox, Drafting, In review, Published, Outdated |
| **Fields** | Owner (a person), Review by (a date), Topic (a choice: Start here, How we work, Customers, Money, Tools) |
| **Labels** | how-to, policy, decision, checklist |
| **Shared with** | The team as editors. New starters as viewers before their first day. |
| **First articles** | "Start here: how this handbook works", "Your first week" with a page for each day, "Who to ask about what" |

### Answers for customers

The questions support hears every week, each answered once and kept right.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lists** | Asked, Drafting, Checked, Published, Retired |
| **Fields** | Product area (a choice), Owner (a person), Last checked (a date), Asked how often (a number) |
| **Labels** | billing, account, how-to, known problem |
| **Shared with** | Support as editors. The whole company as viewers. Switch **Anyone with the link can view** on if customers should read it. |
| **First articles** | The ten questions asked most last month |

**Asked** counts as Not started, **Retired** counts as Done, and the three between count as In progress.

### Engineering notes and decisions

What was decided, what broke and why, and how to do the things done twice a year.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lists** | Proposed, Discussing, Decided, Superseded |
| **Fields** | Area (a choice), Owner (a person), Decided on (a date), Replaces (a card link to the decision it supersedes) |
| **Labels** | decision, how-to, incident, runbook |
| **Shared with** | Engineering as editors, everyone else as viewers |
| **First articles** | The last three decisions people still argue about |

**Decided** counts as In progress, so decisions stay in sight. **Superseded** counts as Done.

## Seven outlines

### A how-to

```md
## When you need this

One or two sentences. Who does this, and when.

## Before you start

- [ ] What you need to have
- [ ] What you need to know

## Steps

1. First step
2. Second step
3. Third step

## If it goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
|  |  |

## Who to ask
```

### Troubleshooting

```md
## What you see

The message, or what happens, in the words people use.

## Why it happens

## Fix it

1. 
2. 

## If that doesn't work

## So it doesn't happen again
```

### A decision

```md
## What we decided

One sentence.

## Why

- 
- 

## What else we looked at

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
|  |  |

## What it costs us

## When to look at this again
```

### A policy

```md
## The rule

In two or three sentences, as you would say it to someone.

## Who it is for

## What to do

1. 
2. 

## Exceptions, and who can agree to one

## Questions people ask
```

### Questions people ask

```md
## Can I …?

Yes, when … No, when …

## How long does … take?

## Who do I ask about …?

## What if …?
```

### A first week

```md
Welcome. This is what happens in your first five days, and who to ask.

## Before you arrive

- [ ] Your account
- [ ] Your kit

## Who to ask

| About | Ask |
|---|---|
|  |  |

The pages inside this one cover each day.
```

Add a page inside it for each day: see [pages inside a page](/knowledge/organise#pages-inside-a-page).

### Notes from a meeting

```md
## Who was there

## What we decided

- 

## What happens next

- [ ] Who does what, by when

## Open questions
```

Turn each line under "What happens next" into a card on the board where the work is done, and mention it here by
its name.

## Save your own

When you have an outline you like, write it into a card and save the card as a
[template](/everyday/templates#save-a-card-as-a-template). A board can keep several: "How-to", "Decision",
"Policy". Each new article then starts with the right headings.
