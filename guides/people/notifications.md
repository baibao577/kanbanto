# Notifications

Kanbanto tells you when something needs you: under the bell, by email, on your computer, and in Telegram if you set that up.

## The bell

The bell at the top right shows:

- comments and descriptions where someone **@mentioned** you,
- new comments and changes on the **cards you follow**,
- **reminders** that have come due,
- being **added** to a board or workspace.

![The bell, with a mention, a comment and changes on followed cards](/images/bell.webp){.medium}

Click one to open the card. **Mark all as read** clears the count.

## Cards you follow

You follow a card when you are part of it: you made it, it is assigned to you, you commented on it, or someone
mentioned you on it. From then on the bell tells you about:

- new comments,
- the card moving to another list,
- who it is assigned to (including when it is given to you),
- its due date or description changing,
- the card being archived or deleted.

Several changes in a row by the same person show as one line, and you are never told about your own changes.

To follow any other card, open it and click **Follow** at the top of the card. The button says **Following** once
you do; click it again to stop.

![Following, ringed, at the top of an open card](/images/follow.webp)

A line under the bell from a followed card has its own **Unfollow**.

Once you unfollow a card, commenting on it again does not follow it again. Being assigned the card does.

Following a card does not include its subtasks: each card is followed on its own.

## The morning summary

Once a day, around 8:00 in your own time zone, Kanbanto emails you:

- cards due today, and overdue ones,
- reminders set for later today,
- mentions you have not seen yet,
- news from the cards you follow that you have not seen yet.

It is only sent on days when there is something to say.

## Reminder emails

A reminder also arrives by email at the moment you set. See [due dates and reminders](/everyday/dates-and-reminders).

## Notifications on your computer

Mentions, reminders and news from the cards you follow can pop up on your screen even when the Kanbanto tab is closed, as long as your browser is
running. Clicking one opens the card.

1. Open **your initials → Account settings → Notifications**.
2. Turn on desktop notifications, and allow them when your browser asks.

This is set per computer and browser, so turn it on wherever you want it.

## In Telegram

Reminders and mentions can also reach you in Telegram, as they happen, through a bot you connected to your own
chat with it. Each bot tells you about the board it is on, your Inbox included
([how](/more/telegram#your-own-reminders-and-mentions)); then choose
what it tells you under **Account settings → Notifications → Telegram**: reminders and mentions are on to start, comments and
changes on the cards you follow are off.

## Choose what you get

**Account settings → Notifications** is where you:

![Notification settings](/images/notifications.webp)

- turn the **morning summary** on or off,
- turn **reminder emails** on or off,
- turn **desktop notifications** on for this computer, and choose which kinds you get there (reminders, mentions,
  cards you follow),
- choose what a **Telegram** bot of your own tells you, once you have one,
- set **your time zone**, which decides when your morning is. It is taken from your browser the first time and can be
  changed.

## Good to know

- You are only notified about boards you are on.
- Being assigned a card tells you under the bell, and you follow it from then on.
- Email needs to be set up by whoever runs your Kanbanto site. If it is not, the bell and desktop notifications still
  work.
