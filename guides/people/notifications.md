# Notifications

Kanbanto tells you when something needs you, in three places: the bell, your email, and your computer.

## The bell

The bell at the top right shows:

- comments where someone **@mentioned** you,
- **reminders** that have come due,
- being **added** to a board or workspace.

![The bell, with a mention](/images/bell.webp){.medium}

Click one to open the card. **Mark all as read** clears the count.

## The morning summary

Once a day, around 8:00 in your own time zone, Kanbanto emails you:

- cards due today, and overdue ones,
- reminders set for later today,
- mentions you have not seen yet.

It is only sent on days when there is something to say.

## Reminder emails

A reminder also arrives by email at the moment you set. See [due dates and reminders](/everyday/dates-and-reminders).

## Notifications on your computer

Mentions and reminders can pop up on your screen even when the Kanbanto tab is closed, as long as your browser is
running. Clicking one opens the card.

1. Open **your initials → Account settings → Notifications**.
2. Turn on desktop notifications, and allow them when your browser asks.

This is set per computer and browser, so turn it on wherever you want it.

## Choose what you get

**Account settings → Notifications** is where you:

![Notification settings](/images/notifications.webp)

- turn the **morning summary** on or off,
- turn **reminder emails** on or off,
- turn **desktop notifications** on for this computer,
- set **your time zone**, which decides when your morning is. It is taken from your browser the first time and can be
  changed.

## Good to know

- You are only notified about boards you are on.
- Being assigned a card does not send a notification. If you want someone to notice, @mention them in a comment.
- Email needs to be set up by whoever runs your Kanbanto site. If it is not, the bell and desktop notifications still
  work.
