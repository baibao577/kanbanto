# Your first board

This takes about five minutes. By the end you will have a board with a few cards on it, and you will have moved one.

## Before you start: get in

Open your Kanbanto site's address in your browser (see [where Kanbanto lives](/start/what-is-kanbanto#where-kanbanto-lives)).

- **New here?** Click **Create an account**, and give your name, your email address and a password. Some sites then
  send an email with a link to confirm your address: click it and you are in. If the site does not let people sign up
  by themselves, you need an invite from someone already on it.
- **Have an account?** Sign in.
- **See "Continue with Google"?** Then your site lets you use your Google account instead, to create an account or
  to sign in: one click, with no password to choose and no email to confirm.

## 1. Click "Create board"

After you sign in you land on the boards page. Every board you can open is here. Click **Create board** at the top
right.

![The boards page, with Create board ringed](/images/first-1-create.webp)

## 2. Give it a name

Type a name, such as "My first board". The rest can stay as it is:

- **Where:** Personal means only you can open it, until you share it. If you are in a team
  [workspace](/people/workspaces) you can pick that instead. Setting up for a team? Personal is fine for trying
  things out; a board can be moved into a workspace later.
- **Start with:** **An empty board** has lists for To Do, Doing and Done and no cards yet (plus a Backlog list that
  is hidden: a line at the right of the board says so, and shows it when you want it). **The example board** is a
  small website-launch plan to explore. **A sales pipeline**, **A support desk**, **Store orders** and **Bookings** are
  [starters](/everyday/fields#start-from-a-ready-made-board): boards for one kind of work, with their own lists and
  fields.
- **Background:** any color or design you like.

Then click **Create board**. You can change all of this later.

![The Create a board dialog, with the name and the Create board button ringed](/images/first-2-name.webp){.medium}

## 3. Click "Add a card"

Your board opens with three lists: **To Do**, **Doing** and **Done**. Under **To Do**, click **Add a card**.

![A new, empty board, with Add a card ringed](/images/first-3-add.webp)

## 4. Type what needs doing

Type the task and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> (or click **Add card**).

![A card's title being typed](/images/first-4-type.webp)

The box stays open, so keep typing to add the next one. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> when you have a few.

![Three cards in To Do](/images/first-5-cards.webp)

::: tip Dates in plain words
If you type a date in the title, Kanbanto shows the date it understood under the box, before you press Enter. "Order
name badges friday 2pm" becomes a card called "Order name badges", due on Friday at 14:00. There is a picture of this
on the [Cards](/everyday/cards#add-a-card) page.
:::

## 5. Drag a card to "Doing"

Press on a card, drag it over the **Doing** list, and let go. A dashed outline shows where it will land.

![A card being dragged from To Do to Doing](/images/first-6-drag.webp)

That is all "starting work" means here. When the work is finished, drag the card to **Done**.

![The card, now in Doing](/images/first-7-moved.webp)

On a phone or tablet, press and hold a card first, then drag.

You can also move a card without dragging. Point at a card and a small **⋯** button appears at its corner (on a phone
it is always there). Click it and pick a list under **Move to**.

## 6. Open a card

Click a card to open it. This is where the detail lives: a description, who it is for (**Assignee**), when it is
due, files and comments. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click outside to close it. Everything else on the card is explained
on the [Cards](/everyday/cards) page.

![An open card](/images/first-8-open.webp)

## Good to know

- **Nothing needs saving.** Changes are kept as you make them.
- **Undo** with <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> on Windows). Deleting and archiving also show an **Undo** button
  for a few seconds.
- **Lists are yours.** Rename one by clicking its name. Add more with **Add another list**.
- On the boards page, a board says **Invited only** when only the people you added can open it. A new Personal board
  is like that, with nobody added yet.
- You can also add a card by pressing <kbd>N</kbd>, or with **New task** at the top.
