# Log time

If you want to know where your hours go, log time on cards. It is optional: boards work the same without it.

## Log from the board

1. Click **Log time** at the top of a board, or press <kbd>L</kbd>.

   ![The top of a board, with Log time ringed](/images/time-1-button.webp)

2. Type part of a card's name and pick it with <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The cards you worked on that day come first.

   ![The log box, with a card being picked](/images/log-1-pick.webp)

3. The same box now asks how long. Type it, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

   ![The log box, with a card picked and a time typed](/images/log-time.webp)

Ways to write a time:

| You type | Means |
|---|---|
| `2` | 2 hours (a plain number is always hours) |
| `1:30` or `1h 30m` | An hour and a half |
| `45m` | 45 minutes |
| `1.5` | An hour and a half |

Anything after the time is a note: `1:30 review with Ben`.

Quick buttons add 15m, 30m, 1h, 2h or 4h, and the box shows how much you have logged that day.

**Another day:** click a day at the top of the box, or press <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd> to step back and forward.

## Log from a card

- Open a card and click **Log** beside **Time**, or
- choose **Log time…** in the card's **⋯** menu.

The box opens with that card already picked.

## See the time on a card

An open card shows its total under **Time**, and the entries behind it: who, when, how long, and the note.

![A card's logged time](/images/card-time.webp){.medium}

A parent's total includes its subtasks' time.

## Fix an entry

On an open card, under **Time**, click an entry's time, day or note to change it. To delete an entry, point at it
and click the **✕** at its end.

You can change your own entries. A board's owner, or an admin of its workspace, can correct anyone's; the entry then
says who edited it.

## Who can log, and who sees it

- People who can edit the board can log time.
- Everyone on the board sees the time on cards.
- Visitors using a public link do not see logged time.

## With your assistant

"Log 2 hours on the homepage for yesterday" works. It only logs what you tell it; it never guesses.
