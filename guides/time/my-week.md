# My week

My week shows your time on every board in one table: a card per row, a day per column.

Open it from the menu under your initials, or from **Open my week** in the log box.

![My week: cards down the side, days across the top](/images/my-week.webp)

## Read it

- The top says how much of the week is logged, against your hours a day (8 unless your team's plan says otherwise).
- Each day's heading shows that day's total. A working day with nothing logged stands out.
- Cards are grouped by board.
- A **dot** in a cell marks a day you worked on that card (moved it, changed it or commented on it) without logging
  time. It is a nudge: did that take time?

## Fill it in like a spreadsheet

Click a cell and type hours: `2`, `1:30` or `45m`. Move between cells with the arrow keys or <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

To log on a card that is not listed, click **Add a card** and pick it.

## Other weeks

Use the arrows beside the dates to move a week back or forward. **This week** brings you back.

## Good to know

- My week is yours alone. Nobody else sees this page.
- Time you log here is the same time shown on the cards: there is only one record.
- Your assistant can read it: "Which days this week have I not logged?"
