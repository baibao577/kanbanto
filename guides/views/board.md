# The Board view

The Board shows your cards in lists, side by side. It is the view most people live in.

![A board with three lists](/images/board.webp)

The grey **Homepage** heading in each list is a card that has subtasks: the cards under it are its subtasks. Click
the heading to open that card. More on this in [tasks inside tasks](/everyday/subtasks).

## Lists

A list is a stage of the work. Each list has a menu, opened with its **⋯**.

![A list's menu](/images/list-menu.webp){.medium}

- **Rename.** Or just click the list's name.
- **Color.** Tints the list's header.
- **Order cards by.** By hand (as you dragged them), or sorted: by priority, due date or title, or by when the
  cards were **created** or last **updated** (newest first). **Reverse the order** turns any of them round: oldest
  first, least important first, Z to A. **Keep this order** makes a sorted order the new order by hand. (*Updated*
  is the last real change to a card: its title, list, dates, description and so on. Dragging it to another place
  doesn't count.)
- **Move left / Move right.** Or drag the list by its header.
- **Cards in this list are…** what the list counts as: **Backlog** (planned, not ready yet), **Not started**, **In
  progress** or **Done**. This is how Kanbanto knows which cards are finished, whatever you name your lists.
- **Archive older cards…** on a done list. See [done cards and the archive](/everyday/done-and-archive).
- **Hide list** takes it off your screen only. A line beside the lists says which are hidden, with a way to show
  them again. New boards start with a hidden Backlog.
- **Delete list…** asks where its cards should go first.

The button beside the **⋯** folds a list into a narrow strip. Click the strip to open it again.

Add a list with **Add another list** at the right.

## Display: how cards look and group

Click **Display**. These choices are yours alone; they do not change the board for anyone else.

![The Display menu](/images/display.webp){.medium}

- **Rows** splits the board into horizontal bands: **A row for each project** (top-level card), **for each parent
  task**, or **for each person**. Useful when several projects share one board.
- **Show** chooses which cards appear:

  | Choice | Shows |
  |---|---|
  | **All tasks** | Every card. |
  | **Only tasks without subtasks** | The cards you actually do, without the parents above them. |
  | **Main tasks only** | One card per piece of work, with its subtasks listed on it. Normally that is the top-level cards; with **A row for each project**, it is the cards directly inside each project. |
  | **Only projects** | Top-level cards only, whatever the rows. |
  | **Up next (ready to start)** | Cards that have not started, have no subtasks and are not waiting on another card. |

- **Group subtasks under their parent** is on to begin with: a parent becomes a heading in each list, with its
  subtasks under it. Turn it off and every card stands alone.
- **Card numbers** shows each card's [name](/everyday/cards#a-card-s-name), like WEB-12, above its title.
- **Where it belongs** shows the parent's name above each card.
- **Subtask checklist** and **Progress bar** show a parent's steps and progress on its card.
- **Card age** adds a small tag to cards nothing has happened on for 3 days or more: the number of days, amber after
  a week, red after two.
- **Show parents only as rows** appears when Rows is set to a row for each parent. It stops the parent from also
  showing as a card.
- **Done lists** shows every finished card, or only recent ones (14 days, or the number you choose).

A dot on the Display button means something is changed from the usual.

## Presets

**Presets** saves the current filters and Display options under a name, such as "Mine this week" or "Review", for everyone
on the board to pick. See [search and filters](/everyday/search-and-filters#presets-save-a-combination).

## Moving things

- Drag a card to another list, or within its list.
- Drag a parent's heading to move its subtasks in that list together.
- Drag a list by its header.
- On a phone, press and hold first.

When a list is sorted (not "By hand"), cards cannot be dragged into a new order inside it. Kanbanto tells you so,
and you can switch back to By hand.

## Good to know

- Long lists show the first 100 cards, with **Show more** at the bottom.
- The background is the board's own; change it under **⋯ → Board background**.
