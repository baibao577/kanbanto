# The Outline view

The Outline is a table of every task, indented under its parent. It is the best view for seeing a whole project at
once, and for tidying up.

![The Outline view](/images/outline.webp)

## What you see

One row per task, with columns for its status, assignee, dates, priority and more. Projects (top-level tasks) are
lightly tinted, and guide lines show what sits under what.

- Click the arrow beside a task to fold or unfold what is inside it.
- Click a row to open the card.

## Change things in place

Click a task's status in the table to move it to another list without opening it. For anything else, click the row
to open the card.

## Rearrange

Drag rows to put them in the order you want.

## Sort and filter

- Click a column's heading to sort by it.
- **Filter** and search work as in the other views.

## Choose what to show

Click **Display**:

- pick the **columns** you want,
- switch between **compact** and **comfortable** rows,
- turn on **Hide done** to leave finished tasks out.

## Add tasks

Use **New task** or <kbd>N</kbd>. To add a subtask, open its parent.

## On a phone

The Outline becomes a nested list, with each task's details underneath it.

## Good to know

- The order of tasks in the Outline is its own. Dragging cards around on the Board does not shuffle the Outline, and
  the other way round.
- Long boards show the first 500 rows, with **Show more**.
