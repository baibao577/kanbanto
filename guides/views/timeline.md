# The Timeline view

The Timeline shows the same cards as bars on a calendar: when each starts, when it is due, and how they overlap.

![The Timeline view](/images/timeline.webp)

## What you see

- Each row is a task. Subtasks sit under their parent; fold or unfold a parent with the arrow beside it.
- A task with a **start** and a **due** date is a bar between them. A task with only a due date is a mark on that day.
- A task with no dates has an empty row: click in the row to give it dates.
- A line marks today.

## Change dates by dragging

- Drag a bar left or right to move it.
- Drag either end to make it longer or shorter.

The card's start and due dates change with it. Undo with <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>.

## Zoom

Switch between days, weeks and months to see the next two weeks or a whole year.

## Hide what is done

**Hide done** removes finished tasks from the Timeline. The Outline follows the same switch (there it is under
**Display**), so turning it on in one hides them in both. A line says how many are hidden.

## Bar colors

A bar takes the color of its status. To pick one yourself, open the card and choose a **Timeline color**.

## When the board belongs to a plan

If your team plans people's time, and this board is linked to a project in the workspace's plan, the top of the
Timeline shows who is booked on the project, at what share of their time, and until when. See
[planning people](/time/planning).

## Good to know

- Search and filters work here too.
- Long boards show the first 500 rows, with **Show more rows**.
- On a phone, scroll sideways through time; press and hold a bar to drag it.
