# The Timeline view

The Timeline shows the same cards by date. It can be drawn two ways, and you switch between them with **Bars** and
**Calendar** at the top:

- **Bars:** a row for each task, with a bar from its start to its due date. Best for work that lasts days: how long
  each thing takes, and what overlaps.
- **Calendar:** each day with the cards that are on it. Best for things that happen on a day: bookings, deliveries,
  posts, deadlines.

Each board remembers which one you last used, on that device.

The bar under the tabs is the same in every view: **Presets**, **Filter** and **Display**. What belongs to the
Timeline alone sits in a row of its own under it: Bars or Calendar, Today, and how much time to show.

![The Timeline view, as bars](/images/timeline.webp)

## Bars: what you see

- Each row is a task. Subtasks sit under their parent; fold or unfold a parent with the arrow beside it.
- A task with a **start** and a **due** date is a bar between them. A task with only a due date is a mark on that day.
- A task with no dates has an empty row: click in the row to give it dates.
- A line marks today.

## Change dates by dragging

- Drag a bar left or right to move it.
- Drag either end to make it longer or shorter.

The card's start and due dates change with it. Undo with <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>.

## Zoom

Switch between **Days**, **Weeks** and **Months**, at the right of the Timeline's own row, to see the next two
weeks or a whole year. **Today** brings today back into view.

## Fold and unfold

The arrow beside a parent folds its subtasks away. The two small buttons in the **Task** heading expand or collapse
everything at once.

## Hide what is done

**Display → Hide done tasks** removes finished tasks from the Timeline. The Outline follows the same switch (it is
in its **Display** too), so turning it on in one hides them in both. A line says how many are hidden.

## Bar colors

A bar takes the color of its status. To pick one yourself, open the card, click **Dates** and choose a **Timeline color**.

## The calendar

![The Timeline as a calendar: a month of bookings](/images/timeline-cal-1-month.webp)

- A card is on the day it is **due**, with its time if it has one. A card with only a start date is on that day.
- A card with a **start and a due date** stretches across its days.
- A card with no date isn't on the calendar. A line under it says how many there are; **Bars** and the Board show
  them.

### How much to show

| | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Month** | The whole month. Up to five cards a day; past that, four and **+ more**, which opens that day |
| **2 weeks** | This week and the next. Up to eight cards a day, then **+ more** |
| **Week** | Seven days side by side, every card |
| **Day** | One day as a list in time order: cards for any time that day first, then by time |

The arrows move to the next or the earlier month, week or day, and **Today** comes back. Click a day's number to
see that day alone.

A card looks the same in all of them: its time, its title in full, its priority, how many of its subtasks are
done, and who it is assigned to. A row of days grows as tall as its cards need.

Days and weeks list their cards in time order. There is no hour-by-hour grid: a card has a moment, not a length.

![A week, with subtasks shown](/images/timeline-cal-2-week.webp)

### Add a card on a day

1. Point at the day and click **+** (in **Day**, use the box under the list).
2. Type a title and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The box stays open for the next one; <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

![Adding a card on a day, with its time in the title](/images/timeline-cal-3-add.webp){.medium}

- A time of day in the title becomes the card's time: "Call Sam 3pm" is added at 15:00, as "Call Sam".
- The card is due that day, assigned to nobody, in the board's first list of work not started.

### Change a card's date

On a computer, drag a card to another day. A card that has a time keeps its time, and a card that lasts several
days moves whole. Undo with <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>. On a phone, open the card and change its date there.

### Subtasks

Out of the box the calendar shows the top-level cards only. Turn on **Display → Subtasks** to also see subtasks
that have a date of their own:

- each is on its own day, and says what it is under ("Shop refit › Painter comes"),
- subtasks without a date never show by themselves: they stay on their card.

A card that has subtasks says how many are done (1/4), with the switch on or off. When subtasks with a date are
hidden, a line under the calendar says how many, with **Show**.

### On a phone

**Month** is the days with a dot for each card; tap a day to list its cards under the month. **Week** and
**2 weeks** are a list, day by day.

![The calendar on a phone](/images/timeline-cal-4-phone.webp){.small}

## When the board belongs to a plan

If your team plans people's time, and this board is linked to a project in the workspace's plan, the top of the
Timeline (as bars) shows who is booked on the project, at what share of their time, and until when. See
[planning people](/time/planning).

## Good to know

- Search, filters and **Hide done** work in both, bars and calendar.
- With **Hide done** on, rows still fold and unfold. While you search or filter, what is found is shown unfolded.
- In Bars, long boards show the first 500 rows, with **Show more rows**.
- In Bars on a phone, scroll sideways through time; press and hold a bar to drag it.
